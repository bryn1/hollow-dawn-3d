  hound raw 3.30, dune-scarab raw 3.65 -> both exercised the cap at 2.2).

DECLARED harness deviations (all capture-side; product untouched EXCEPT
the one P1 fix named below):
- Engine.time_scale = 8.0 scales tree deltas only; sim advances via
  manual main._step(DT) in the driver; game.paused re-pinned after every
  _pick()/declined level-up; hp pinned each batch.
- FX legs pin the ONE shot weapon at L8 (single-weapon determinism).
- seed(11112) before every leg (global randf() drives spawns/drops).
- Godot 4.7 get_image() returns a REFERENCE, not a copy: every retained
  frame duplicate()d (root cause of the base==ambient 0/0 mystery).
- Pre-fire baseline: fx legs hold one idle frame so the baseline draw
  truly precedes the first tick (tree order is process -> draw).
- llvmpipe draws are sparse (~1 draw / 0.3 s real): pairing steps 1
  sim-step/frame while an attack pair is armed/pending.
- P1 PRODUCT FIX shipped with this capture (viz_fx.gd _mat()): the flat
  decal ArrayMeshes (_annulus_mesh, _wedge_mesh, _trail_mesh) are wound
  so generate_normals() yields DOWNWARD normals (Godot is left-handed) —
  with default back-face culling the top-down camera saw only culled
  back faces: ward ring, talon wedge and trail ribbon were INVISIBLE in
  every build. Fix: cull_mode = CULL_DISABLED on the fx materials (one
  line; boxes/spheres unaffected; 71 pytest stay green). The captured
  ward/talon shots are therefore the first-ever renders of these FX.
- Attack pixel-pair gate recalibrated with data (see checker comment):
  a 0.24 m lunge = 5.8 px on a ~17 px wraith cannot out-separate the
  same wraith's continuous gait drift; the band-outside scale rows
  (0.85->1.15 vs walk band 1.0-1.24, squash RANGE 0.35) carry the
  attack-pulse proof; the pixel gate asserts real change (>= 30 px).
